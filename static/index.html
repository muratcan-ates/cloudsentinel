How this page works

four steps

The product is four rooms in a line, and the desk is the doorway to all of them. Each step below opens the room that does it.

  1. 1 · Signals surface Cost, security and fraud run through one detector, and a day that leaves its own baseline becomes a signal. watch →
  2. 2 · Evidence and triage The analyst reads the evidence behind one signal and the recommender proposes options, both citing what they used. investigation →
  3. 3 · The hand decides A proposal stays inert until you accept or reject it, and the verdict is sealed into a hash-chained ledger. decision desk →
  4. 4 · Results measured Acceptance, time to decide and saved run rate are counted from that ledger — arithmetic, never generation. intelligence →
