the orchestration console — 4 seats, 1 answer at a time

Four agents sit on this panel and each one may do exactly one job. Pick a seat, ask a question, and read the answer next to the evidence it leaned on. Every reply carries a badge naming where it came from — a demo model, a live model, or the deterministic fallback — because a console that hides that is not worth trusting.
